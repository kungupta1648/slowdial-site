THE PROBLEM

People are already paying hundreds of dollars to get away from this.

The Light Phone sells for a few hundred dollars, does almost nothing, and people buy it anyway — because a plain rectangle that can't reach Instagram at 11pm is worth that much to them. Most people won't make that trade. They still need Maps, banking, their boarding pass, a QR code at the register.

Slow Dial is the free version of that feeling — on the phone you already own. Nothing to buy, nothing to give up. Just a different front door to it.

HOW IT WORKS

Three small changes to your home screen.

1

Your home screen becomes a keypad.

Nine keys, styled like an old backlit LCD. It sits over a matching wallpaper until the widget itself disappears.

2

Speed dial, but for people.

Keys 1–9 are the people you actually talk to — a message or a call, one press. Apps live one key away, not scattered across a grid.

3

Distracting apps get a dial tone.

Instagram isn't on the keypad. To open it, you spell its name on the keys and wait out a short call. Every call today makes the next one longer.
